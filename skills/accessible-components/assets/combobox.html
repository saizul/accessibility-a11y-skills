Combobox (autocomplete list)

Editable combobox with list autocomplete. Focus stays in the input; the highlighted option is exposed with aria-activedescendant. Prefer a native <select> or <input list> with <datalist> when they are enough.

Type a name, then use the arrow keys to choose.
